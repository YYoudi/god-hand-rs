# Historical lab: development continues in God Hand PC

Final editorial transition, 9 November 2026. This independent MIT Rust/Bevy lab
is **paused, not achieved**. Its source, pending work, tests, original screenshots,
failed comparisons, old pages and Git history remain preserved. It is not the
active implementation of the PC port and will receive no new PC progress entries.

The single active entry point is [God Hand PC](https://yyoudi.github.io/god-hand-pc/),
with its [source repository](https://github.com/YYoudi/god-hand-pc),
[roadmap](https://yyoudi.github.io/god-hand-pc/roadmap.html),
[reports](https://yyoudi.github.io/god-hand-pc/reports/index.html) and
[selected comparisons](https://yyoudi.github.io/god-hand-pc/comparisons.html).
It is a separate GPL continuation of LasVola, requires a personal ISO and does
not distribute the game. A complete accurate port/remaster is not yet validated.

Selected earlier PC reports and entries were migrated editorially, retaining
their original dates, revisions, attribution and limitations. Selected already-
reviewed native comparison screenshots were copied byte-for-byte with unchanged
hashes and provenance; there was no research-directory or media-folder sweep.
Migration is not new runtime evidence. Original MIT notices and separate image
rights remain explicit in the new repository. Existing copies here remain valid
historical evidence and may contain now-superseded workflow descriptions.

This repository remains unarchived to preserve its current Pages configuration
and links; archiving consequences have not been assumed safe. The original PC
goal remains active in god-hand-pc. No new Rust work or history rewrite is part
of this transition.
